Deep Restoration & Scalp Spa

A restorative sanctuary for dry, damaged strands and tired scalps using bioactive keratin, collagen, and herbal steam therapy.

⏱️ Duration: 60 — 75 mins 💰 Price: $27 — $60 🌿 100% Silicone-Free & Cruelty-Free 💆 Scalp & Shoulder Massage Included

Rebuild From Within

Restore elasticity, softness, and natural brilliance

Environmental stress, hot styling tools, and chemical processing strip hair of essential keratin proteins and lipids. Our intensive multi-stage repair protocol penetrates deep into the cortex to seal broken disulfide bonds and smooth down outer cuticles without heavy artificial silicone build-up.

The 4-Stage Protocol

The restorative journey

  • 1

    Scalp Micro-Exfoliation & Detox

    Gentle salicylic acid and tea tree scrub gently clears sebum, product residues, and hard water minerals.

  • 2

    Bio-Lipid & Keratin Infusion

    High-concentration hydrolysed keratin and hyaluronic acid serum massaged directly strand by strand.

  • 3

    Micro-Mist Ultrasonic Steam Bath

    Warm nano-steam opens hair cuticles gently to lock nutrients into the core without heat damage.

  • 4

    Cold Cuticle Sealing & Acupressure Finish

    Cool organic citrus rinse to seal in shine followed by a 15-minute soothing neck, shoulder, and upper back massage.
