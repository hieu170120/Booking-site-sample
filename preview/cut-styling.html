Service Detail & Preview

Cut & Styling Experience

A bespoke haircut meticulously shaped around your bone structure, hair volume, and everyday routine.

⏱️ Duration: 45 — 60 mins 💰 Price: $18 — $35 💇 Recommended Stylist: Lan Nguyen / Minh Tran 🛡️ 7-Day Free Touch-up

The Philosophy

Effortless beauty that lasts between visits

At Lumière, we don’t believe in one-size-fits-all haircuts. We analyze your hair’s natural fall, cowlicks, texture, and facial contours before cutting a single strand. The result is a flattering, low-maintenance shape that looks just as good when you wash and style it at home.

Step-by-step Process

What your appointment includes

  • 1

    1-on-1 Consultation & Scalp Assessment

    Discuss your goals, inspiration photos, and daily routine while we examine your hair texture and scalp condition.

  • 2

    Aromatherapy Wash & Acupressure Scalp Massage

    Relax with an organic botanical shampoo, cooling mint rinse, and gentle pressure-point neck & head massage.

  • 3

    Precision Architectural Haircut

    Layering and weight removal tailored precisely to enhance your natural movement and facial angles.

  • 4

    Blow-out Styling & Home Care Coaching

    Finished with luxury leave-in serum and personalized tips on how to blowdry and maintain the volume at home.
