Aftercare Guide

How to maintain your color at home

When can I wash my hair after coloring?

We advise waiting at least 48 hours before the first shampoo to allow the botanical color molecules to fully set inside the hair cuticle.

Do I get free touch-ups if the tone is too light or dark?

Yes, all color services come with our 7-day satisfaction guarantee for free toner adjustments.
