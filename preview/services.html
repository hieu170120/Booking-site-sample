Frequently Asked Questions

Common Inquiries About Our Services

Do I need to book in advance?

We recommend booking at least 1–2 days ahead to secure your preferred stylist, especially for weekend slots.

What is the 7-day guarantee policy?

If you feel your haircut or color tone needs any adjustment within 7 days of your visit, we will happily fine-tune it completely free of charge.

Can I consult with a stylist before deciding on a service?

Yes! Every booking starts with a complimentary 10-minute hair analysis and lifestyle consultation.
